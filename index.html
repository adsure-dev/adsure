Do I need to know marketing to use AdSure?

No. If you can create an ad on Meta or Google, you can use AdSure. Every problem is explained in plain words, with the fix.

Do I have to give you my ad account password?

No. You set up your ad on AdSure's own screen, which works like Meta or Google Ads. You never share your ad account password with us.

Will AdSure get me more sales?

No one can honestly promise sales. AdSure helps you find costly mistakes before you spend, avoid common reasons ads get rejected, and know your real profit per sale.

How is this different from Meta or Google's suggestions?

Their suggestions mostly tune settings inside their own platform. AdSure also checks your website page, tracking links, creative quality, profit after COD returns and Indian ad rules, for both platforms in one place.

How is this different from asking ChatGPT or another AI?

Chatbots can give a different answer each time and can make up rules. AdSure's score comes from 248 fixed checks, each with a real source. Same ad, same score, every time.
