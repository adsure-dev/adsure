What AdSure does

AdSure helps you check your Meta (Facebook and Instagram) and Google ads before and while you spend. Depending on your plan, this includes:

  • a review of your ad creative, ad text, landing page and targeting by our AI and our ad experts;
  • an AI score out of 10 with a list of fixes;
  • full expert reviews, checked by one of our experts before you get them;

AdSure gives you advice. It does not run, publish, pause or change your ads, and it never needs a login to, or access to, your Meta or Google ad accounts. You decide what to change and you make the changes yourself.
